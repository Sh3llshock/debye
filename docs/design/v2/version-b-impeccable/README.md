Made by following [impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) (v4.10): `SKILL.md`, `reference/craft-floor.md`, `mode-operate.md`, `layout.md`, `typeset.md` and `colorize.md`. PNGs: `staff-case-detail.png`, `staff-cases-list.png`, `partner-job.png`, `whatsapp-flow.png`. Tokens: `tokens.json`.

## Mode and world

- **Mode: Operate.** The staff and partner pages are working tools. The skill says known patterns win and brand shows in precise details.
- **What the world contributes** (the skill allows only four things): type (Public Sans, a plain civic sans), palette (a navy shell with a blue action colour), density (tighter, table-like), and **one signature move**.
- **Signature move:** the case lifecycle drawn as a service line (Reported → Consent → Dispatched → Booked → Done → Invoiced → Closed). It sits across the top of the case and job pages and appears in compact form in each row of the cases list.

## Rules applied

- **Colour does real jobs** ("a grey screen with one accent is the category default"): a committed navy rail, a tinted ground, and white work areas. Blue `#2E5BBA` marks actions, selection and the current station. Red, amber and green appear only on state chips and the decision row.
- **Bans from the craft floor:**
  - no eyebrow or kicker labels above headings
  - no hero-metric template (the "14" card is gone)
  - no same-size icon-heading-text cards as page structure, and no nested cards
  - no side stripe on rounded boxes
  - no gradient text
  - no monospace used as a "technical" costume
  - no glass or glow
- **Elevation declared once:** borders only, no shadows.
- **Layout:** distinct work areas instead of card grids. On the case page: activity (the main path), the conversation beside it, an inspector with properties in sections divided by rules, and the partner choice as a table. The approval sits inside the table as a "needs decision" group, not a modal or a hero card.
- **Type:** one family with a fixed role scale (30 / 18 / 16 / 15 / 14 / 13), weights 400–600, tracking no tighter than -0.01em, tabular figures.
- **Copy (clarify):** controls name their action ("Offer visit times", "Send 2 times to Luka", "Daten weitergeben" / "Nicht weitergeben", "Termine ansehen"). Status text says what is happening ("Times offered to resident", "waiting for Luka").
- **Standard controls only:** a segmented filter, a table, and a week grid with Free / Busy / Offered cells for picking visit times. No costume of a tool.

## What changed vs v1

- The cream, aubergine and serif look is replaced by a navy shell and a single sans.
- Cards are replaced by work areas with rules.
- The vertical lifecycle bar is now the horizontal service line, with timestamps under each station.
- The partner slot picker is now a week grid that also shows when the installer is busy.
- WhatsApp: buttons name the action, and the header reads "Debye · reltix, Antwortet sofort".
- The Debye mark is the new mascot avatar.
